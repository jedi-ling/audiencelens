# Persona Ethics

AudienceLens personas may contain gender, age range, region, and MBTI-like traits.

These fields exist to enrich voice, context, and perspective diversity. They must not be used as deterministic predictors of behavior.

## Incorrect Use

- “Female players like story.”
- “Male players like strategy.”
- “INFP users will always prefer emotional games.”
- “ENTJ users only care about efficiency.”

## Correct Use

- “This persona’s feedback is driven by low patience, Steam browsing habits, and a decision rule requiring clear gameplay within 15 seconds.”
- “MBTI is used only to shape tone, not as a behavior cause.”
