# Evaluator: Persona Differentiation

Check whether each persona has a distinct view.

Pass examples:
- Steam browser focuses on capsule, tags, screenshots.
- Visual-first player focuses on mood, UI cohesion, screenshot shareability.
- Core player focuses on system depth and replayability.
- Toxic reviewer attacks overclaiming and prototype feel.

Fail examples:
- Every persona says “make onboarding clearer.”
- Persona names differ, but behavior is identical.
