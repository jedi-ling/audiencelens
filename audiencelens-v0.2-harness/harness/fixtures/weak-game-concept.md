# Star Destiny Online

## One-liner

A massive sci-fi open world RPG where players explore the universe, fight enemies, collect resources, build civilizations, trade, craft, socialize, and shape the fate of the galaxy.

## Core loop

Players explore planets, collect items, fight monsters, upgrade gear, build bases, unlock stories, and join factions.

## Target platform

PC and mobile.
