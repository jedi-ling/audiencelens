# Cyber Scavenger

## One-liner

A vertical mobile clicker where an overworked cyber employee mines compute tokens while avoiding platform risk control.

## Core loop

Tap different AI platform nodes to generate compute tokens, manage energy, battery, risk credit, and money. Survive 7 in-game days and hit the weekly KPI.

## Target platform

Mobile / mini program.

## Current stage

Concept + early UI prototype.

## Test focus

- First impression
- Core loop clarity
- Risk control tension
- Whether players understand why they should rotate nodes
- Whether the satire is funny enough
